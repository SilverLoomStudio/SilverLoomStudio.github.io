Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android games of SilverLoom Studio collect, why, and what you can do about it. It covers GEONAUT (com.silverloomstudio.geonaut) and Daily Path (com.silverloomstudio.dailypath), installed from Google Play, including test versions.

Who is responsible

SilverLoom Studio, an independent game developer based in Prague, Czech Republic, is the controller of the data described here. Contact: silverloom.studio@gmail.com.

In short

  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account of ours.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • The games send usage statistics (without your name or contact details) and crash reports to Google Firebase, so we can fix errors and improve the games. They do not use the advertising ID or the Android ID.
  • If you use Google Play Games (achievements and leaderboards), Google keeps them under your Play Games profile.
  • There are no ads and no purchases in the games today. If we add them, we will update this policy first.
  • We never sell your data.

What is collected and why

1. Game progress — on your device only

Your progress and settings (levels, stars, records, equipment, sound and vibration) are saved in the game's local storage on your phone. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

2. Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

To learn how the games are played and where they are too hard, too easy or too slow, the games send events such as: a level or descent started and finished (mode, level, depth, time, stars), screens opened, in-game currency spent, a result shared, the answer «yes / maybe / no» in the feedback form, and smoothness and sound statistics of a session. Firebase adds technical data: an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate country or region derived from the IP address.

Not collected: the advertising ID, the Android ID, precise location, and any text you type. Advertising features of Google Analytics are switched off. Data is kept for 14 months.

3. Crash and error reports — Firebase Crashlytics

When the game crashes or meets an error, a report is sent with the error details, device model, Android and app version, the version of the system WebView, a Crashlytics installation identifier and the state of the device at that moment (for example free memory). Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Statistics and crash reports are not sent from test devices of Google Play's automated pre-launch check and from developer builds.

4. Achievements and leaderboards — Google Play Games Services

GEONAUT uses Google Play Games Services; Daily Path will use them in a later version. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game receives your Play Games player ID and public profile (gamer name and avatar), and sends your achievements and scores to Google. Google stores them under your Play Games profile; who can see your profile and scores is set in your Play Games settings. You can play without Play Games.

5. Feedback you send

The «Feedback to the developer» screen prepares a text with your game statistics. It leaves the phone only if you share or copy it yourself, for example to an email or a messenger. We then receive what you send and use it only to answer you and improve the game.

6. Google Play

Google Play processes data about installs, updates, purchases and test programs under Google's own privacy policy. We see only summary statistics in Google Play Console and the email addresses of testers who join our test programs.

Legal basis

Usage statistics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the games working and improving them (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and your consent where the law requires it. Play Games features: performance of the service you choose to use (Art. 6(1)(b)). Feedback: your consent and our legitimate interest in answering you.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to silverloom.studio@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. The games do not tell us who you are, so we may be unable to link statistics or crash reports to you; if so, we will say so in our answer. You can also file a complaint with a data protection authority — in the Czech Republic it is the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

Without asking us you can: reset the advertising and analytics settings of your Google account, manage your Play Games profile in the Play Games app or at play.google.com/games/profile, and delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Children

The games are made for adults and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our developer accounts is protected by two-step verification.

Changes

We will update this page before any new data collection starts (for example ads or purchases) and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes on Google Play.
